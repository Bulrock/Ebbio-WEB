Ebbio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Ebbio is a vocabulary app that schedules reviews using the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve. This policy covers the Ebbio mobile apps for Android and iOS as well as the web version at bulrock.github.io/Ebbio-WEB. The short version: Ebbio has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising, and everything you create stays on your device.

Data Ebbio collects

None. The developer of Ebbio operates no server and receives no data from the app. There is no sign-up, no user account, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create is stored locally — in the app's private storage on mobile, and in your browser's IndexedDB storage on the web:

  • your word cards — the word, its definition, your translation hint, and its review schedule;
  • your settings — course languages, interval steps, mistake handling, interface language.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it (see Backups) or unless it is included in a device backup you have enabled in your system settings. Uninstalling the app — or clearing the site data in your browser — deletes it.

Data sent to third parties

Ebbio contacts external services only while you are creating or editing a card, and only with the single word or phrase you typed. Training, reviewing and browsing your dictionary work fully offline.

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
Wiktionary (Wikimedia Foundation) The word or phrase you typed, and the language of your course. To fetch a dictionary definition.
MyMemory (Translated S.r.l.) The word or phrase you typed, and your language pair. To suggest a translation hint.

These requests contain no identifier, no account and no device information beyond what any HTTP request necessarily reveals to the receiving server (such as your IP address and the app's user-agent string). Each service handles that request under its own privacy policy: the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy and the MyMemory Privacy Policy. If a lookup fails, the fields are simply left empty for you to fill in by hand — you can also skip autofill entirely and use Ebbio without any network access.

Text-to-speech

Pronunciation is played through the speech engine built into your device or browser. Depending on which engine you have configured, your operating system or browser may process the word through that engine's own service. Ebbio passes the word to it and receives audio back; it makes no connection of its own.

Notifications

Review reminders are scheduled on the device itself — by the operating system on mobile, and by the page's service worker on the web. No push server is involved, and nothing about your cards is sent anywhere in order to deliver them. You can decline or revoke the notification permission at any time; Ebbio remains fully usable without it.

Backups

Ebbio can export your cards and settings to a JSON file. Where that file goes — a chat, a cloud drive, your downloads folder — is entirely your choice, and the developer receives no copy. Importing works the same way in reverse, from a file you pick yourself.

Hosting of the web version

The web version is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the requests your browser makes for those files; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Ebbio itself sends nothing to GitHub, sets no cookies and runs no tracking script.

Children

Ebbio is a general-audience learning app. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because no data ever reaches the developer, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. You are in full control: delete individual cards inside the app, uninstall it, or clear the site data in your browser to remove everything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
